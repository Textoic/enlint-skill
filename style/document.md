# The style guide: document structure

This pass changes the shape of the passage: how many paragraphs there are, what
sits in each, what order the ideas come in, and what formatting carries them. It
does not police word choice, and it does not rewrite a sentence for its own sake.
Other passes do that.

## The principles that bear on structure

1. Be direct with the information you want to communicate. Don't be roundabout.
2. Hedging your arguments only weakens them.
3. Choose substance over style, even if it contradicts any of these rules.

## Avoid bold announcements

Don't use headings or bold around 1 phrase to introduce each "key concept", "main idea", "load-bearing component" and such, and definitely do _not_ say you are going to list these main ideas.

Human writing is lumpy and variable, not everything fits neatly into exactly 1 paragraph for each "main idea", some ideas need longer to develop, and others shorter.

So instead, remove all the bolded introductions on paragraphs, and let the ideas flow naturally from one paragraph to the next. Use as many paragraphs to develop an idea as necessary, and shorten the less important ideas into 1 or 2 sentences, maybe even at the end of another paragraph.

## Avoid the 'topic sentence' structure

Don't use a rigid pattern to make a point on a subject like the common "topic sentence -> elaboration -> example -> wrap-up" structure.

Human writing is direct, changes structure for different points, and may start directly with an example of why something is important without elaborating before/after, or elaborating. Or simply state the point cleanly, in 1 sentence, with little follow-up or elaboration. Pick the structure that best fits each case, **not** the same structure for everything.

## Don't balance arguments

If the text is in favor of a position, you don't need to balance every arguemnt with a counterexample just to have a balanced view of something, because then you stop being persuasive.

Drive the point you want to make, include only defenses against most likely attacks, don't be balanced.
